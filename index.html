Cloud-Based IoT Indoor Air Quality Monitor

  • Multi-point sensor network measuring CO₂, temperature, humidity and airflow (SCD41 + FS3000).
  • Designed and fabricated 3 custom PCBs: Raspberry Pi 5 to dual TCA9548A I²C multiplexers, P82B715 bus extenders for long sensor runs, and regulated sensor nodes.
